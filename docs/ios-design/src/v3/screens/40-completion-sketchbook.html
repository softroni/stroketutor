Developer notes

Purpose

§32 "At completion, do not grade the drawing… the final action is to help the learner keep evidence of progress." The headline is the plan's own sentence, templated from lessons[].subjectNoun; the page card is the evidence; the green button keeps it.

Layout

  • Full-screen cover from the player (state == .finished): no nav bar, no tab bar, no close. The three actions are the only exits.
  • The page: .page-thumb 3 : 4, white, with a soft gold frame (2 pt gold at 55 % inside, 6 pt --gold-soft outside). It takes whatever height the text leaves — about 300 × 400 pt here — so the drawing is the largest thing on screen. The chip--gold "Drawn · 12 Sep" overlaps its foot.
  • Headline 30/800 centred, at most two lines. Lina: .face--lg 48 pt + one 17/500 line at 70 % ink, no bubble. Two .tiles in a 12 pt grid: 24/800 value, 13/600 label.
  • Bottom: primary 64 pt, secondary 60 pt, "Not now" 48 pt text; 12 pt between. The secondary says only "Next lesson" so a long localised title can never break it.

States & interactions

  • default — mid-path. "Add to sketchbook" → sk-capture primer (straight to the camera when video access is already authorised); "Next lesson" → hp-preview; "Not now" → hp-path. Completion is written before this screen appears, so "Not now" loses nothing.
  • path-done — path.lessonIds.last == lesson.id. Instead of one page, a 5 × 2 contact sheet of all ten drawings (the newest ringed gold, check badge). Secondary becomes "Choose another path" → hp-paths. Cells show the learner's photo when one exists, else the tutorial strokes.
  • No auto-dismiss, no timer §31. One .success haptic on appear; no sound.

What changed in v3

  • White page card instead of the cream canvas-frame; gold marks the finished drawing (frame and chip), as on the path nodes.
  • Lina is a round portrait with a plain line, not a figure with a speech bubble: quieter, and it lines up with the narration chip in the player.
  • Facts are .tiles; buttons are the tactile 64/60 pt set. Duolingo shape (hero, headline, tiles, one primary) without XP or accuracy.
  • v1's separate sk-path-done screen is folded into this one as a variant: the sheet of ten is the graduation, no extra screen or trophy §39.

Accessibility

Developer notes

Purpose

§32 "Photograph the physical page after a tutorial. Store locally and privately by default." Fast enough to do every time, honest enough that nobody is surprised later about where the picture went.

Layout

  • primer — 56 pt icon tile, 30/800 title, one 17 pt line, then three promises in a .list--soft with green check tiles (each row 60 pt). Shown before AVCaptureDevice.requestAccess so "Don't Allow" is an informed choice. "Choose from Photos" needs no permission at all.
  • camera — dark full-bleed. Guide 300 × 400 pt centred, 1.5 pt dashed at 38 % white, 30 pt corner brackets; hint pill top; lesson ghost 64 pt bottom-right. Bar: Photos · 76 pt shutter · flash, 108 pt + safe area.
  • review — photo and lesson side by side, both 3 : 4 in a 12 pt grid, so the learner compares rather than judges. One soft button, "Fix corners" once the page has corners (found or placed) and "Crop" while it is the photo as taken; it opens the corner editor. Retake / Keep as a 50-50 row, Keep green.
  • saved — the green chip is the whole celebration. The page as it now looks in the sketchbook (196 pt), title, path, full date, the privacy line. Primary "Next lesson", secondary "Open your sketchbook" → sk-book, "Done" → hp-path.

States & interactions

  • Skip primer when video access is .authorized. When .denied, no camera: one line "Camera access is off for StrokeTutor" with a button to Settings; "Choose from Photos" still works.
  • Shutter → freeze → review. Retake keeps the session alive. Keep writes image → fsync → record → navigates; on failure stay on review with the photo in memory.
  • Auto-crop (VNDetectDocumentSegmentationRequest, 300 ms budget, confidence > 0.8) finds the paper's four corners and "Your page" shows it straightened with CIPerspectiveCorrection; until then, and when nothing is found, it shows the photo as taken. It never blocks saving: Keep saves whatever "Your page" shows.
  • One edit: four-corner perspective correction, which crops and straightens in one gesture (a phone photo of paper is keystoned, not rotated, so a ± 15° straighten could not square it). The corner editor opens full screen on the photo as taken: four round handles (24 pt dot, 48 pt hit target) on the detected corners, or inset 8 % when none were found; the quad outlined in green, the rest dimmed; a loupe above the dragged handle. Handles stay on the photo and stop where the quad would stop being convex. Cancel · Reset (to the detected corners) · Done (corrects off the main thread). No filters, no enhancement.
  • Storage: Application Support/Sketchbook/<uuid>.jpg (long edge 2048, q 0.8, GPS stripped) plus a 400 px thumb; SwiftData record stores the file name. Not the Photos library by default; "Also save to Photos" is a Settings opt-in. Included in device backup.

What changed in v3

  • The primer's promises are a grouped .list--soft with check tiles, not a bordered card; the icon tile replaces a chip.
  • Review and saved show the page as a photographed sheet on a warm desk (.sk-shot) inside a white 3 : 4 card, the same cell the sketchbook grid uses, so the learner recognises it there.
  • Saved is a calm static screen with a green chip rather than a toast over a card; tactile 64/60 pt buttons throughout.

SwiftUI mapping

 ├ CameraView                           // AVCaptureSession(.photo), UIViewRepresentable preview

 │    └ CornerEditor(image:corners:)     // fullScreenCover; PageCorners → CIPerspectiveCorrection

Accessibility

  • Guide and ghost are .accessibilityHidden; the shutter is always enabled ("Take photo"). Photos: "Choose a photo of your page". Flash: "Flash, automatic".
  • Each corner handle is its own element ("Top-left corner", its position as its value) with the custom actions Move up / down / left / right, 2 % a step.
  • Announce "Page detected" when segmentation succeeds. Reduce Motion: 120 ms cross-fade instead of the shutter flash.
  • Primer, review and saved scale with Dynamic Type; the review pair stacks above .accessibilityMedium.

Open questions

  • Offer "Also save to Photos" once on saved, or only in Settings? A checkbox converts better; Settings is calmer.
  • Two attempts on one sheet: one entry with two photos, or two entries? The schema assumes one image per entry.

Developer notes

Purpose

Layout

  • Scrolling body. 36/800 title with the chip--gold count on the right, the same chip as on Learn (a fact, not a score).

States & interactions

Developer notes

Purpose

One kept page, shown large, with the lesson it came from. §32 "Associate the image with lesson, path, and completion date." The only forward action is to draw it again; the rest is quiet.

Layout

  • Nav bar: back to the sketchbook, title "Sketchbook", share at the trailing edge (44 pt targets). Pushed from the tab; the tab bar hides for the detail.
  • Scrolling body: the .sk-shot full width (353 × 470 pt), then title 24/800, "Trees · Lesson 1 of 8 · 16 steps" 15/600, the full localised date 13/600, and the chip--gold "Drawn" on the right.
  • Note field: a 60 pt --surface row, 17/500, pen icon, placeholder at 40 % ink. Multi-line, grows with content; saves on end editing.
  • "Draw it again" is the 60 pt secondary (not green: the learner already finished this). Share and Delete are 48 pt text buttons in one row, Delete in --danger.

States & interactions

  • default — tap or pinch the photo → full-screen zoomable viewer, swipe down to dismiss. "Draw it again" → hp-preview for this lesson.
  • Share (row or nav) → system share sheet with the 2048 px JPEG: a private export to Messages, Mail, Files or AirDrop. No in-app sharing, no link, no post.
  • delete — always confirmed with a destructive alert whose body says what survives: the photo and note go, the LessonProgress row stays; deleting never re-locks a lesson. Then modelContext.delete(entry), remove both files, pop to sk-book. Cheap softening if wanted: a 10 s Undo toast before removeItem.
  • Data: one SketchbookEntry (id, lessonId, pathId, completedAt, imageFile, note). "Lesson 1 of 8" from paths[pathId].lessonIds.firstIndex; drop "of 8" if the lesson left the path.

What changed in v3

  • No "Your page / The lesson" toggle: the photo is the page, full width, and the lesson is one tap away in "Draw it again". Less chrome, more drawing.
  • The note field, reserved in v1, now has a UI: one calm row under the facts.
  • Share and Delete are visible quiet buttons instead of an overflow menu, so an older learner never has to discover an ellipsis. The alert is the shared .alert-overlay.
