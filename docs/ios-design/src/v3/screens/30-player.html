Selecting “Drawing” restarts the CSS draw-on and the pencil tip. In this mockup the step is always 3 of 5 (the door), so “I drew it” loops back to the same step drawing; in the app it begins step 4. The paper is 393 × 500 pt in every state; the sheet never changes height.

Developer notes

Purpose

Watch → draw → confirm → continue §4. A step plays, the app stops, the learner draws on paper and taps I drew it. No countdown, no auto-advance §31: .awaitingUser is only left by advanceToNextStep(), only from the primary. M7 wraps the existing PlayerViewModel and DrawingCanvasView; it does not replace them.

  • Tactile buttons: 64 pt primary with a 4 pt darker edge; 64 pt round quiet buttons with a 2 pt border and edge; instruction 24/800 instead of 22/700.

Accessibility

  • Reading order: instruction → hint → paper (“Step 3 of 5, the door, drawing/drawn”, value = instruction) → primary → ◀ ↻ → chip → thumbnail → header. Announce “Step drawn. Draw it on your paper, then tap I drew it.” on the transition.
  • Labels: × “Close lesson”; ⋯ “More options”; chip “Narration on/off. Double tap to mute/turn on.”; thumbnail “Reference photo. Double tap to expand.”; primary value “Lina is still drawing” while pending.
  • Dynamic Type: instruction relativeTo: .title2, uncapped; the sheet grows and the paper yields down to 300 pt, then the column scrolls. Button labels capped at .accessibility2. Contrast: 15/600 at 55 % ink on white is 5.9:1; the pending ring's green on white 3.4:1 at 20/800 (large text).
  • Reduce Motion: the stroke still draws (it is the lesson) at 0.6× duration; the wave freezes; the pencil tip drops its trail; fill change is a 0.15 s crossfade.
  • Every control ≥ 60 pt; the chip's hit area is padded to 60 pt. No hidden gestures §31.

Developer notes

Purpose

The same screen on its side, for subjects wider than they are tall (a cat's face, a car, a street). Nothing added, nothing removed: the learner turns the phone when the drawing looks small — no setting, no prompt §31.

The player stays fully rendered under the 50 % scrim. The resume state is on hp-preview (variant resume, Home & paths) — “Leave” takes you there.

Developer notes

Purpose

Leaving mid-lesson is normal; most lessons are 3–5 minutes but life is not §7. The sheet makes one promise — your place is kept — and the app keeps it. Not a guilt gate §30: no “give up?”, no streak warning, no count of what is unfinished.

Layout

  • .sheet with .presentationDetents([.height(300)]), radius 28, grabber, 20 pt gutters, safe-bottom + 12. Title 24/800 centred, body 17/400 at 55 % ink, balanced.
  • Keep drawing is the primary and it is ink, not green — green is reserved for moving forward Duolingo; staying is the safe default, not progress. Leave is a 17/700 text button, no destructive red: nothing is destroyed.

  • The resume state is no longer mocked here; it is the preview's job (hp-preview variant resume) and lives with that screen.

Accessibility

  • Modal: focus moves to “Leave this drawing?”; the player behind is .accessibilityHidden(true). Escape gesture = Keep drawing.
  • Leave's label carries the promise: “Leave this lesson. Your place is kept.”
  • Detent becomes .medium at accessibility sizes so the two buttons never crop.
