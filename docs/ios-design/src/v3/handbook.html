Design handbook · v3

This is the third design of the StrokeTutor iOS learner app: the same product, the same flows and the same rules as the first document, with a new visual system built to stand next to Duolingo and Simply Draw on a home screen. White paper, one bold green, tactile buttons, a visible path of lesson nodes, bigger type. Every screen here is interactive: Continue walks through onboarding, a node opens its lesson, Start drawing opens the player, I drew it moves it on.

What does not change

  • The ten rules of the first handbook: watch → draw → confirm → continue; the paper is the product; adult tone; reality stays in view; one loud button; big and readable; paths unlock in order; calm progress; local and private; the existing player is kept and wrapped.
  • Narration and Lina, as specified in the first document (voice pipeline, playback rules, bounded use of the character).
  • The app map, the on-device data model, the content files the app reads, the accessibility checklist and the milestone mapping. Open questions carry over.
  • Screen ids and flow links, so the two documents can be read side by side: index.html#hp-home and v3.html#hp-home are the same screen in two designs.

Implementation notes for SwiftUI

  • Pressed edge. A ButtonStyle that draws a rounded rectangle offset 4 pt below the label in the edge colour and moves the label down 4 pt while isPressed. One style, three colour sets (green, white, ink).
  • Nodes. LessonNode(state:, drawing:): a 84 pt circle, the lesson's finished drawing rendered from its tutorial JSON at 60 pt (white strokes on gold when done), the halo as a scaling Circle().stroke with .repeatForever unless Reduce Motion.
  • The zig-zag. A VStack of rows, each offset by an alternating x in [−54, 54] pt, label on the outer side. Keep the tap target the node itself.
  • White paper. tutorial.style.backgroundColor stays in the data; the player ignores it in favour of Theme.paper = .white unless the lesson sets an explicit colour (colour-fill lessons in v2 schema).
  • Hero banner. A LinearGradient green card with a 6 pt edge; the drawing thumbnail is a white rounded rectangle with the tutorial rendered into it.
