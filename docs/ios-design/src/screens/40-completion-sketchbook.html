Developer notes

Purpose

§32 “At completion, do not grade the drawing. The application has already structured the task to produce a satisfying result. The final action is to help the learner keep evidence of progress.” The headline is the plan’s own example sentence, re-templated: "Your \(lesson.subjectNoun) is finished." + “Add it to your sketchbook.”

Duolingo keeps the completion shape — hero, one headline, a tile row, one full-width primary — and drops what we do not measure: no XP, no accuracy, no “Flawless”, no share-for-a-reward.

Layout

  • Full-screen cover, no tab bar, no nav bar, no close button: the only ways out are the three actions. The player pushes this when PlayerViewModel.state == .finished.
  • Hero: 230 × 230 pt .canvas-frame centred, paper #FAF7F0, radius 32. It is the tutorial’s own strokes at lineWidth from the JSON, drawn on once.
  • Headline 28 pt/34 heavy, centred; sub-line 17 pt regular at 55 % ink.
  • Two tiles, equal width, 12 pt gap: value 24 pt heavy, label 12 pt uppercase at 55 % ink. Two facts only, both honest and both derived, never invented.
  • Lina: lina--xs (40 × 48) plus one short line in a .speech bubble. One sentence, no exclamation mark, no praise adjective.
  • Bottom: primary 68 pt green, secondary 60 pt, text 48 pt. Secondary drops to 17 pt because lesson titles are long — measure, and fall back to “Next lesson” alone if the localised title does not fit one line.

States & interactions

  • default — mid-path. Secondary names the next lesson in the same path.
  • path-last — path.lessonIds.last == lesson.id. Secondary becomes “See the whole path” → sk-path-done. Everything else is identical; do not add a trophy, a badge or a sound.
  • If the next lesson belongs to a path the learner has not started, show “Choose what to draw next” → hp-paths instead.
  • “Add to sketchbook” → sk-capture variant primer (or straight to camera if AVCaptureDevice.authorizationStatus(for: .video) == .authorized).
  • “Not now” → hp-path. Nothing is lost: see Data.
  • No auto-dismiss, no timer §31.

Data

  • Completion is written before this screen appears, and does not depend on the photo. On the last “I drew it”, the Progress store writes
    LessonProgress { lessonId, pathId, completedAt: Date, stepCount, elapsedSeconds, replayCount }
    and unlocks path.lessonIds[index + 1]. A learner who taps “Not now” is fully credited.
  • Tile 1 = tutorial.steps.count (5 for simple-house). Tile 2 = measured wall-clock time in the player, rounded to the nearest minute and clamped to ≥ 1 min; if the learner left and resumed, use summed foreground time, and if that is unreliable (> 30 min) show the lesson’s estimate instead. Never show a time the learner will read as a verdict on speed.
  • Headline noun comes from a new catalog field lessons[].subjectNoun (“house”, “cottage”, “street scene”, “palm tree”). Do not derive it from the title by string surgery — it will not localise.
  • Hero strokes: all strokes of all steps of shared/Tutorials/<id>.json, at full opacity §39 (“derive the final sketch from all completed tutorial strokes rather than a separate final SVG asset”).
  • Lina’s line is authored per lesson: lessons[].completionLine, optional; with no line, drop the bubble and keep the spacing.

Voice & narration

  • Optional: one short recorded line at Assets/Voice/<lessonId>/complete.m4a, played once, only if narration is on. It says the same words as the bubble. No chime, no fanfare.
  • Respect the silent switch (category .ambient), duck other audio, never interrupt a call. If the learner muted Lina in the player, she stays muted here.

Accessibility

  • VoiceOver order: headline → sub-line → tiles → Lina → actions. The hero is one image element labelled “Your finished Simple House drawing”, not six paths.
  • Tiles are one element each: “5 steps”, “4 minutes of drawing” (spell the unit out for speech).
  • Dynamic Type: all text scales. Above .accessibilityMedium the two tiles stack vertically and the hero shrinks to 180 pt; the screen becomes a ScrollView so nothing clips. The three buttons never shrink below 60 pt.
  • Reduce Motion: no draw-on; the finished drawing is present on appear. The shared @media (prefers-reduced-motion) rule does this in the mockup; in SwiftUI check UIAccessibility.isReduceMotionEnabled before starting the stroke animation.
  • Contrast: green primary #1C8F5C with white text is fine at 24 pt heavy. Do not tint the headline gold.

Developer notes

Purpose

§32 “Photograph the physical page after a tutorial. Store locally and privately by default.” This flow has to be fast enough that a learner does it every time, and honest enough that they are not surprised later about where the picture went.

Layout

  • primer — three plain promises, each 17 pt, each true. Shown before AVCaptureDevice.requestAccess(for: .video) so a “Don’t Allow” is an informed choice, not a reflex. “Choose from Photos” is offered at equal prominence; it needs no permission at all.
  • camera — full-bleed dark. Guide frame inset 46 pt with 30 pt corner brackets and a 1.5 pt dashed outline at √2 page proportion; hint pill at the top; ghost of the lesson drawing 76 pt bottom-right at 62 % white. Bottom bar 3-up: Photos · 76 pt shutter · flash. Cancel is the nav close.
  • review — the photo and the tutorial drawing side by side at the same height, both 3 : 4, so the learner compares rather than judges. One quiet “Crop & straighten”. Retake and Keep are a 50/50 row, Keep is the green primary.
  • saved — the toast is the only celebration. The pair underneath is “Next lesson” and “Done”; the next lesson’s title is deliberately not in the button here — it is already one tap away on hp-preview, and a long localised title will not fit 24 pt heavy. The card below is literally the sketchbook cell that now exists, so the learner learns what a sketchbook entry looks like before opening the tab.

States & interactions

  • Entry: from sk-complete. Skip primer when video access is already .authorized. When it is .denied, do not show the camera at all: show a short line “Camera access is off for StrokeTutor” with a button to UIApplication.openSettingsURLString, and keep “Choose from Photos” working.
  • Shutter → freeze → review. Retake returns to the live session (do not tear it down). Keep writes the entry and goes to saved.
  • Auto-crop is optional and must never block saving. Run VNDetectDocumentSegmentationRequest (iOS 15+, falls back to VNDetectRectanglesRequest) on the captured buffer; if it returns a quad with confidence > 0.8, pre-fill the crop and perspective-correct. If it fails, times out (budget 300 ms) or the learner disagrees, keep the full frame. Never show an error about it and never refuse a photo because no page was detected — a page drawn on a cluttered desk is still the learner’s drawing.
  • Only two edits exist: crop and straighten (rotation ± 15°). No filters, no brightness, no “enhance”. §32 the record is the page, not a product shot.
  • Swipe-down / Cancel from camera returns to sk-complete, which still offers the photo later; the entry can also be added from sk-book later via the lesson row.

Data & storage

  • App container, not the Photos library. Image at Application Support/Sketchbook/<uuid>.jpg plus a 400 px grid thumbnail at <uuid>-thumb.jpg; create the directory with FileManager.url(for: .applicationSupportDirectory, …, create: true). No UIFileSharingEnabled, so it is invisible in Files.
  • Record (SwiftData, one store, no CloudKit container in M8): 
    @Model final class SketchbookEntry {
  @Attribute(.unique) var id: UUID
  var lessonId: String      // "simple-house"
  var pathId: String        // "houses"
  var completedAt: Date
  var imageFile: String     // "<uuid>.jpg" — RELATIVE, never an absolute URL
  var note: String?         // reserved; no UI in M8
}
    Store the file name: the container path changes on reinstall and on restore from backup, so an archived absolute URL is a broken image on a new phone.
  • Why not PHPhotoLibrary by default: (1) the camera roll is the most shared surface on the phone — shared albums, family devices, AirDrop, screen mirroring — and a beginner’s practice page is exactly what they may not want surfacing there; (2) it needs NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription and a second permission prompt at the best moment of the lesson; (3) we would have to persist a PHAsset.localIdentifier, and a delete or an iCloud “optimise storage” eviction in Photos silently empties a sketchbook page we promised to keep; (4) §32 says local and private by default. Offer Settings → “Also save to Photos” (off by default) which additionally writes to a “StrokeTutor” album; the container copy remains the source of truth.
  • Privacy strings (Info.plist), written in the app’s own voice: 
    NSCameraUsageDescription
  "StrokeTutor uses the camera only to photograph your finished
   drawing for your own sketchbook. Photos stay on this iPhone."
NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription      // only if "Also save to Photos" is on
  "StrokeTutor saves a copy of your finished drawing to your photo
   library when you turn that on."
    PhotosPicker reads out of process, so no NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription is needed for “Choose from Photos”. Do not add it.
  • Image sizing: downscale the long edge to 2048 px and write JPEG at quality 0.8 (≈ 350–700 KB per page; 200 pages ≈ 100 MB, acceptable). Use CGImageSourceCreateThumbnailAtIndex with kCGImageSourceThumbnailMaxPixelSize: 2048 and kCGImageSourceCreateThumbnailWithTransform: true so the full-resolution buffer is never decoded and EXIF orientation is baked in. Strip GPS before writing — we do not need to know where the learner draws.
  • Backup: Application Support is included in iCloud/iTunes device backups by default, so a restored phone restores the sketchbook. Do not set isExcludedFromBackup. Explicit cross-device sync is deferred §37§39 (“Cloud sync/accounts for sketchbook, if ever desired”) — say so in st-about rather than implying sync.
  • Write order on Keep: image file → fsync → insert the record → navigate. If the write fails, show the failure in place and keep the learner on review with the photo still in memory.

Accessibility

  • The guide frame and brackets are decorative: .accessibilityHidden(true). Framing precision is a sighted affordance, so a VoiceOver user must never be blocked by it — the shutter is always enabled and auto-crop never rejects a frame.
  • Shutter: “Take photo”. Photos: “Choose a photo of your page”. Flash: “Flash, automatic” with a value trait.
  • Consider adding, for VoiceOver only, the system-style spoken framing help already present in Camera; at minimum announce “Page detected” when the segmentation request succeeds.
  • Dynamic Type: primer and review scale fully; the camera chrome uses fixed sizes like the system camera, but its two labels (“Photos”, “Flash auto”) scale to .large max and truncate with a full accessibility label.
  • Reduce Motion: no shutter flash animation; a 120 ms cross-fade to review instead.
  • Colour: the hint pill is white on 42 % black over live video — add the blur so it passes contrast over a bright page.

  • Tapping a cell opens sk-entry for that page; tapping an unphotographed cell opens that lesson’s preview instead.
  • The primary points at the path the learner is most likely to return to: the one they drew in most recently and have not finished (“Continue the Trees path”), otherwise the first unstarted path (“Start the …”). If every path is finished it becomes “Back to your paths” → hp-home, and Lina’s line changes to one sentence about drawing without a lesson.
  • Shown once automatically. Do not re-present it on every launch; store pathCompletionSeen[pathId].
  • No confetti, no sound, no haptic beyond a single .success notification feedback on appear.

Data

  • Trigger: on completing lesson n, if every id in paths[pathId].lessonIds has a LessonProgress row, present this after sk-complete (via its “See the whole path” secondary, variant path-last).
  • Cells: for each lessonId in path order, the most recent SketchbookEntry for that lesson, else the tutorial’s stroke set.
  • Date range: min/max(LessonProgress.completedAt) across the path. Same month collapses (“6–28 September 2026”), different months do not (“6 September to 12 October 2026”).
  • Photo count: entries with a readable image file. Recount, do not cache — a deleted page must change the sentence.
  • Lina’s closing line is authored per path: paths[].graduationLine. It must name a real-world next step, never praise the learner.

Voice & narration

  • Optional single recorded line at Assets/Voice/paths/<pathId>/done.m4a, played once and only if narration is on, saying exactly the sentence in the bubble.

Accessibility

  • The contact sheet is a grid of buttons: “Your Simple House, 3 September, lesson 1 of 10”. The unphotographed cell reads “Barn, lesson 6 of 10, finished, no photo”.
  • At 64 pt wide the cells are below the 60 pt comfortable target in one dimension; they are 85 pt tall, and every cell is also reachable from sk-book. Above .accessibilityMedium the grid drops to 3 columns and the screen scrolls.
  • Dynamic Type: the title, the factual line and Lina’s line scale fully; the grid re-flows via an adaptive layout (minimum: 60) rather than a fixed column count.
  • iPad: 10 columns in one row, capped at 720 pt content width, with the text block beside it in landscape.
  • Reduce Motion: cells appear together; no staggered fade-in of the sheet.

Open questions

  • Should this also be reachable any time from the path detail screen (a “See the whole path” row), or only once at completion? Reachable is kinder; it also risks becoming a trophy case.
  • Ten cells is a happy case. What does a path of 12 look like — 4 × 3, or 5 + 5 + 2? An adaptive grid answers it, but the creator should see a 12-lesson mock before this ships.
  • If the learner photographed almost nothing (1 of 10), the contact sheet is mostly pen drawings. Is that still a good moment, or should the screen switch to a single sentence and the path title?
