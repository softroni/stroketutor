# Food & Treats: what Lina says

The words of a path are planned together, so ten lessons in a row do not say the same sentence ten
times, and do not sound like ten different teachers either. This is the plan for the ten
`food-treats` lessons (2026-09-20), written before any of them is authored. It follows
[fruits-words.md](fruits-words.md); the model is still the creator's own Strawberry.

## Rules

1. **US English.** Color, center, gray.
2. **Say what is being drawn, first.** "Every donut needs a hole." "The top bun is a wide dome."
   A step that opens with only "Start at the top" tells a child where the pen goes and not what
   they are making. Then, if it helps, where the pen starts and one familiar picture (a hammock, a
   letter V, a hill).
3. **One or two short sentences, under about twelve words each, 110 characters in all.** Calm, no
   exclamation marks, no praise, no jargon, no sizes. `lessons quality` warns `long-instruction`.
4. **Link steps the way a teacher talks:** "Now", "Next", "Then", "Last". About one step in three.
5. **No sentence is shared word for word with any other lesson, in this path or in Fruits, except a
   plain color step** ("Color the crust orange." is fine anywhere).
6. **Counts are the teaching points of this path; say them.** Five sprinkles, two scoops of a
   bite, three and three lines on the cone, four folds, five pepperoni, eight fries, five waves,
   eight pearls, three noodles. The step that finishes a count says the total.
7. **Color steps name the thing and the color.** Plain is the default ("Color the cone orange.");
   at most one varied frame in a row ("The icing is pink."). On the two scenes (ramen, tray) a
   color step may carry two or three things, largest first.
8. **Intros each open differently and end by handing over, in different words.** Outros are one or
   two short sentences about *their* drawing, never about getting it right.
9. **Titles stay short and plain:** "Draw the hole", "Color the icing". Titles may repeat across
   lessons; they are labels, not speech.
10. **Where the trace gives a different grouping, keep the voice of these lines and adapt the
    count.** Anything changed is reported back, and all ten scripts are read side by side before
    they go to the creator.

## The ten scripts

### donut

(Trace: one outer circle, one wavy icing line, the hole, five closed sprinkles. The outer circle
and the icing are each too long for one line: split both.)

- intro: "Who wants a donut? This one has pink icing and five sprinkles. Have a look at how it's made, then make one of your own."
- outer-left: "A donut is one big circle. From the top, curve round the left side to the bottom."
- outer-right: "Now close the circle. From the top again, curve round the right side."
- hole: "Every donut needs a hole. Draw a small circle in the center."
- icing-right: "Next, the icing. Its edge is wavy. From the top, draw two soft bumps down the right."
- icing-bottom: "Carry the wavy edge round the bottom. One bump hangs lower, like a drip."
- icing-left: "Last, two bumps up the left side. They meet the top, and the icing is closed."
- sprinkles-first: "Sprinkles go on last. Each is a tiny bar with round ends. Draw three."
- sprinkles-rest: "Two more, each leaning a different way. That makes five."
- colors: "Color the dough orange." / "The icing is pink." / "Color two sprinkles yellow and two blue." / "The last sprinkle is purple."
- outro: "One donut, iced and sprinkled by you. Nobody else's looks quite like it."

### popsicle

(Trace: one closed body line with the bite in it, two grooves, the stick as one open line.)

- intro: "On a hot day, nothing beats a popsicle. Somebody already took a bite out of this one. See it take shape, then draw your own."
- left-side: "The popsicle is a tall block with a round top. From the top, curve left and go straight down."
- bottom-right: "Turn along the bottom, then go straight up the right side. Stop before the top."
- bite: "Here's the bite. Draw two little scoops, side by side, back to where you began."
- grooves: "Add two short lines, side by side, in the lower half. They touch nothing."
- stick: "Now the stick. From the bottom edge, go down, round the end, and back up."
- colors: "Color the popsicle red." / "The stick is cream."
- outro: "A popsicle with a bite gone already. Better finish yours before it melts."

### ice-cream-cone

(Built 2026-09-20 from the first picture, re-converted with `--min-area 12` so the thin crosshatch traces whole; the grid is uneven, as drawn. Script: cone sides, two scalloped skirts, the bottom
scoop's two visible sides, the top dome, three and three crosshatch lines.)

- intro: "Two scoops, please. We'll build this ice cream from the cone up. Watch it stack, then it's your go."
- cone-left: "Begin with the cone, a long letter V. Draw the left side straight down to the point."
- cone-right: "Then the right side, sloping down to meet it at the point."
- bottom-skirt: "The first scoop sits on the cone. Draw its wavy skirt, three bumps across the cone's top."
- bottom-sides: "Now the scoop's two sides. Each curves up from the skirt and leans in."
- top-skirt: "The second scoop has a skirt too. Three bumps, resting on the first scoop."
- top-dome: "Finish the scoops with a big dome, from one end of that skirt to the other."
- hatch-left: "Give the cone its waffle pattern. Draw three slanted lines leaning left."
- hatch-right: "Three more lean the opposite way. Little diamonds appear."
- colors: "Color the cone orange." / "The bottom scoop is pink." / "Color the top scoop light green."
- outro: "Two scoops and not a drop spilled. What flavors did you pick for yours?"

### cupcake

(Trace: the wrapper as one open line, four folds, three frosting tiers from the bottom up.)

- intro: "It's somebody's birthday somewhere, so let's draw a cupcake. Wrapper first, then a big swirl of frosting. Watch once, then you're the baker."
- wrapper: "The wrapper is a cup with slanted sides. Go down the left, along the bottom, and up the right."
- folds: "Wrappers have folds. Draw four lines down it, fanning out a little."
- bottom-tier: "Now the frosting, in three puffy layers. The widest sits on the wrapper like a cushion."
- middle-tier: "Stack a smaller puff on top of it."
- top-tier: "The last puff is the smallest. Sweep it up to a soft point, like a little wave."
- colors: "Color the frosting pink." / "Color the wrapper blue."
- outro: "Swirl and all, that cupcake is yours. All it needs now is a candle."

### pizza-slice

(Built 2026-09-20 from the first picture, which has NO drip: the drip sentence is unused and the objective still names a drip. With a new picture, `cheese-left` gains its second
sentence; the rest holds for either picture.)

- intro: "Pizza night. We're drawing one slice with five rounds of pepperoni. Watch it get built, crust first, then you take over."
- crust-top: "Begin with the crust. Draw a long arch across the top, like a rainbow."
- crust-bottom: "Bring it back underneath with a second arch. The ends are round, like a sausage."
- cheese-left: "The cheese hangs down in a big letter V. From the crust's left end, head down toward the tip." + with the drip: "Halfway, let the line bulge into one drip, then carry on to the tip."
- cheese-right: "From the crust's right end, draw straight down to meet it at the tip."
- pepperoni: "Pepperoni next. Draw two circles under the crust." / "Two more below them, a little closer together." / "One last circle near the tip. That's five."
- colors: "Color the crust orange." / "The cheese is yellow." / "Color all five pepperoni red."
- outro: "One slice, hot from your pen. Now, who gets the first bite?"

### fries

(Trace: fries overlap and share ink, so lines run on from one fry into the next. Cut at every
junction and rebuild each fry as its own line before grouping. Carton first, front three, back five.)

- intro: "Fries taste best straight from the carton. Watch them pop up, count to eight, then fill a carton of your own."
- carton-top: "Start with the carton. Its top edge is a shallow dip, like a hammock."
- carton-body: "From the left end, slant down and in, go along the bottom, and slant back up."
- front-middle: "Three fries stand in front. Each is a tall thin box with soft corners. Draw the middle one."
- front-sides: "Add one on each side of it, standing on the dip."
- back-center: "Taller fries peek out behind. Draw the center one: up, over and down."
- back-gaps: "Put one in each gap beside it. Stop where they meet the front fries."
- back-ends: "The last two lean out at the far ends. That makes eight."
- colors: "Color all the fries yellow." / "Color the carton red."
- outro: "A full carton, all eight fries standing tall. Try not to get hungry looking at it."

### burger

(Trace: top bun dome, five seeds, the lettuce as one closed line to split into its top and its
wavy bottom, two short cheese ends, the patty with the cheese corner, the bottom bun to split.)

- intro: "Let's stack a burger one layer at a time: bun, lettuce, cheese, patty, bun. Watch the stack grow, then build yours."
- top-bun: "The top bun is a wide dome. From the left, arch up and over to the right."
- seeds: "Scatter five sesame seeds on the bun. Each is a tiny teardrop."
- lettuce-top: "Lettuce pokes out on both sides. Draw a flat line under the bun with a round end each side."
- lettuce-waves: "Its bottom edge is wavy. Count five soft waves across."
- cheese: "A slice of cheese comes next. Draw a short line down at each end."
- patty: "The patty is a long shape with round ends. One corner of cheese melts over it, a letter V."
- bottom-bun: "Last layer, the bottom bun. A flat top, round corners and a gently curved base."
- colors: "Color both buns orange." / "The patty is brown." / "Color the lettuce green." / "The cheese is yellow, and the seeds are cream."
- outro: "That's a proper burger, stacked by you from bun to bun. Order up."

### boba-tea

(Built 2026-09-20 from the first picture: SEVEN pearls, four then three, outlines from the color-edge trace. The lesson says "four small circles" and "Seven pearls in all."; the lines below are for a picture with eight.)

- intro: "Boba tea has chewy pearls at the bottom and a fat straw on top. See how the cup goes together, then draw one for yourself."
- cup: "Draw the cup first. Slant down the left side, go along the bottom, and slant up the right."
- rim: "The lid's rim is a thin band with round ends. Lay it across the top of the cup."
- dome: "A dome sits on the rim. Draw half a circle, like a hill."
- straw: "Poke in the straw. It's a long thin box, leaning right, with a flat top."
- pearls-bottom: "Now the pearls. Draw five small circles in a row along the bottom."
- pearls-top: "Three more rest on top of them. Eight pearls in all."
- colors: "Color the tea cream." / "The rim is blue." / "Color the dome gray." / "The straw is pink." / "Color every pearl brown."
- outro: "Pearls, straw and all, your boba tea is ready. Give the straw stripes if you like. It's your drink."

### ramen-bowl

(Trace: the bowl's lines stop where the seaweed and the chopsticks stand in front of them, so the
bowl is drawn with two gaps and the things in front fill them. The yolk has no outline in the
picture; its line comes from the color-edge trace. At most twelve steps: foot with the body, yolk
with the egg, colors in fours.)

- intro: "A bowl of ramen has a lot going on, so we'll take it slowly: bowl, toppings, noodles, chopsticks. Watch it fill up, then start your own bowl."
- soup-front: "Begin with the soup, a wide flat oval. Draw its front edge in two curves, left then right."
- rim-back: "Draw the back of the oval, and the rim just above it. Both stop where things stand in front."
- body: "Now the bowl itself. From each end of the rim, curve down and under. Add a small foot."
- seaweed: "A sheet of seaweed stands in the left gap. Draw a tilted box with soft corners."
- chopsticks: "Two chopsticks rest in the right gap. Each is a long thin box leaning out."
- egg: "Float half an egg at the front left. Draw an oval, and a circle inside for the yolk."
- noodles: "Noodles next. Draw three wavy lines across the soup, each with three waves."
- onions: "Three small circles on the right are slices of green onion."
- colors: "Color the bowl red." / "The soup is yellow." / "Color the seaweed dark green and the onions light green." / "The egg is cream with an orange yolk. Color the chopsticks brown."
- outro: "Every noodle and topping in that bowl came from you. It looks worth slurping."

### snack-tray

(Built 2026-09-20 from the first picture: five fries ("Two fries stand in front, three are tucked behind."), the cheese corner drawn with the patty, colors in three steps. The lines below are the plan for a corrected picture. Front things first, as on
the Fruit Bowl: burger, boba, then the fries behind both, then the tray under everything. At most
twelve steps.)

- intro: "Lunch is served. Burger in front, fries behind, boba on the right, all on one tray. See what overlaps what, then serve up your own."
- burger-bun: "The burger goes down first, front left. Draw its top bun and three seeds."
- burger-middle: "Under the bun go wavy lettuce and a slice of cheese with one corner hanging."
- burger-base: "Then the patty and the bottom bun."
- boba-cup: "The boba stands front right. Draw the cup, slanting in toward the bottom."
- boba-lid: "Add the rim band, the dome and the leaning straw."
- boba-pearls: "Five pearls sit in a row at the bottom of the cup."
- carton: "The fries hide behind both. Draw only the parts of the carton you can see."
- fries: "Three fries in front, three tucked behind. Stop each line where it meets something."
- tray: "Slide a tray under everything. Draw its outer edge, then one more line just inside it."
- colors: "Color the tray gray." / "Buns orange, patty brown, lettuce green. Cheese and fries yellow." / "The carton is red. The cup and the seeds are cream." / "Finish the boba: blue rim, gray dome, pink straw, brown pearls."
- outro: "Tray's up. You drew a whole meal, and that's Food and Treats finished. Time for a real snack."
