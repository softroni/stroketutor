# Fruits: what Lina says

The words of a path are planned together, so ten lessons in a row do not say the same sentence ten
times, and do not sound like ten different teachers either.

**Rewritten 2026-09-24.** The first Fruits words (2026-09-19/20) were the path's first try: most
steps opened with only where the pen goes ("Start at the top.", "Go back to the top.", "Start in
the dip."), four intros opened "Today we're drawing …", four handed over with "Watch how it comes
together, then it's your turn.", and the outros were "There it is …" again and again. The creator
did not like them and asked for the voice of the Plants (trees) and Food & Treats paths, which
kids enjoy for their variety. All ten lessons were reworded and Lina recorded them again. Drawings,
step ids and step order did not change.

## What the Plants and Food & Treats words do, and these now do too

1. **Intros hook first.** A fact, a scene or a question a child knows ("Cherries grow in pairs …",
   "Monkeys love them …", "Cut an orange across the middle and you find a wheel inside …"), then
   what the drawing is made of, then a hand-over that fits the fruit ("… then pick one of your
   own.", "… then peel off one of your own.", "… then hang a pair of your own.").
2. **Steps say what is being drawn, first**, then where the pen goes, with one familiar picture
   (a boat, a light bulb, a button, an egg standing up, a letter V). Never "Start at the top." alone.
3. **Counts are taught and the step that finishes one says the total:** five seeds, ten grapes,
   eight pieces of orange, six strawberry seeds, four and four pineapple lines.
4. **One or two short sentences, 110 characters at most** (`lessons quality` warns
   `long-instruction`). Calm, no exclamation marks, no praise inside steps, US English.
5. **Link steps like a teacher:** "Now", "Then", "Back to …", "One more …", about one in three.
6. **No sentence is shared word for word with any other lesson of any path**, except plain color
   steps ("Color the leaf green."). Checked by script against every published voice manifest.
7. **Color steps name the thing and the color**; plain by default, at most one varied frame in a
   row ("The stripe is light green.").
8. **Outros are about their drawing and end with a smile:** a joke, a question or a picture
   ("Hang them over your ear and you've got cherry earrings.", "Just don't slip on the peel.",
   "Would you eat them one at a time, or all at once?"). Never about getting it right.
9. **Titles stay short and plain:** labels, not speech; they may repeat across lessons.

## The ten scripts

Step ids are the lessons' own. Intros and outros are Lina's written lines (`voice lines set <id>
lesson-intro|lesson-outro`); every step speaks its instruction.

### watermelon-slice

- intro: "On a hot summer day, nothing beats a cold slice of watermelon. It's a triangle with a curved bottom and a stripy rind. Watch the slice take shape, then serve yourself one."
- left-side: "A watermelon slice starts as a tall triangle. From the top point, slide straight down to the left."
- right-side: "Back to the point, and slide down to the right the same way."
- bottom: "The bottom is curved. Join the two corners with a line that dips low, like a bowl."
- rind-top: "Now the rind. Draw a curve across the slice, a little above the bottom."
- rind-bottom: "One more curve fits between those two. That makes a thin stripe."
- seeds-top: "Watermelons are full of seeds. Draw two small teardrops near the top, pointing up."
- seeds-bottom: "Three more teardrops sit in a row underneath. That's five seeds."
- flesh: "Color the inside pink."
- rind: "Color the rind dark green."
- stripe: "The stripe is light green."
- seeds: "Color all five seeds black."
- outro: "Pink inside, green outside and five black seeds. You can almost hear the crunch."

### apple

- intro: "Apples grow on trees, and this one is ready to pick. It's a round apple with a dip on top, a stem and one leaf. Watch it come around, then pick one of your own."
- left-side: "An apple is a fat circle with a dip on top. From the dip, round the left side down to the bottom."
- right-side: "Now the right side. Start in the dip again and round it down until the two ends meet."
- stem: "Poke a short stem up out of the dip. Up, over its little top, and back down."
- leaf: "A leaf hangs off the stem. Swoop out to a point and back, like a little boat."
- vein: "Give the leaf a vein, one line from the stem toward the point."
- red: "Color the apple red."
- green: "The leaf is green."
- brown: "Color the stem brown."
- outro: "One shiny apple with a leaf on top. Will you eat it, or give it to your teacher?"

### pear

- intro: "A pear is shaped a bit like a light bulb, small on top and round and heavy at the bottom. Watch the curves swell out, then draw a pear of your own."
- left-side: "The pear starts narrow at the top. Go down the left side, then bulge out wide and round to the bottom."
- right-side: "Now the right side, the same shape turned around. Its bottom meets the first one."
- stem: "The stem leans a little to the left. Draw it up from the top, round the end and back down."
- leaf: "A leaf sticks out to the right, high on the stem. Out to a sharp tip and back again."
- vein: "Now one straight line through the middle of the leaf."
- pear-green: "Color the pear light green."
- leaf-green: "Color the leaf green."
- stem-brown: "The stem is brown."
- outro: "Round at the bottom, narrow at the top. That pear looks juicy enough to drip."

### cherries

- intro: "Cherries grow in pairs, two on one stem, like best friends holding hands. Watch the pair come together, then hang a pair of your own."
- front-cherry: "The front cherry is a circle. From the top, curve down its left side."
- front-side: "Then round the right side, back to where you began."
- back-cherry: "The second cherry hides behind the first. Draw only the part that peeks out on the left."
- stems: "Each cherry has a thin stem. Draw both up from the tops until they meet at one point."
- leaf: "A leaf sprouts where the stems meet. Swing out to the right, to a point, and back."
- vein: "Draw a curved line inside the leaf, from the stems toward the tip."
- red: "Color both cherries red."
- green: "Color the leaf green."
- outro: "Two cherries sharing one stem. Hang them over your ear and you've got cherry earrings."

### banana

- intro: "Monkeys love them, and so do lunchboxes. A banana is two long curves that meet at the ends, like a moon lying on its back. Watch it bend, then peel off one of your own."
- top-edge: "The top of the banana is a long, gentle curve. Dip down in the middle and rise up to the right."
- bottom-edge: "The belly curves deeper. From the left end, swing down low and up to meet the top line."
- cap: "The stem is a little square cap on the left end. Up, across and back down."
- tip: "The other end has a tiny dark tip. Draw a short hook to close it."
- ridges: "Bananas have edges along their skin. Draw two long lines inside that follow the belly."
- yellow: "Color the banana yellow."
- brown: "The stem and the tip are brown."
- outro: "One yellow banana with a brown stem. Just don't slip on the peel."

### strawberry

- intro: "Strawberries wear their seeds on the outside, and a leafy green hat on top. We'll draw the hat first, then the berry. Watch it once, then it's all yours."
- left-leaves: "Start with the leafy hat. Draw a pointed leaf curling up to the left."
- right-leaves-2: "Now its twin on the right, curling up the other way."
- right-leaves: "A short stem pokes up between the two leaves. Up, across the top and down."
- middle-leaf: "One leaf hangs down in the middle, a narrow letter V."
- leaf-edges: "Finish the hat with the bottom edge of the leaves, one on each side, out to the tips."
- berry: "The berry is shaped like a heart. Curve down from the leaves to a soft point, and back up."
- red: "Color the berry red."
- seeds: "Color the six seeds black."
- green: "The leaves and the stem are green."
- outro: "Six seeds, a leafy hat and one juicy berry. That's the best strawberry in the patch."

### orange-half

- intro: "Cut an orange across the middle and you find a wheel inside, with eight juicy pieces. Watch the wheel fill in, then cut an orange of your own."
- peel-left: "The orange starts as a big circle. From the top, round down the left side."
- peel-right: "From the top again, round down the right side to close it."
- inside-left: "Just inside it goes a second circle. Draw its left half first."
- inside-right: "Then its right half. The thin ring between them is the peel."
- middle: "Right in the center, draw a little circle the size of a button."
- spokes-plus: "Four spokes go out from the button: up, down, left and right. Stop before the ring."
- spokes-x: "Put one more spoke in each gap, slanting out. Now the wheel has eight."
- tops-right: "Each piece needs a round top. Go down the right side, bumping from spoke to spoke."
- tops-left: "Keep going up the left side until all eight pieces are closed."
- segments: "Color all eight pieces golden yellow."
- peel: "Color the peel orange."
- rest: "The space around the pieces is cream, and so is the button."
- outro: "Eight juicy pieces in one round wheel. Give it a squeeze and you'd have orange juice."

### grapes

- intro: "Grapes grow in bunches on a vine. Ours has ten grapes piled into a triangle, with a leaf and a curly tendril on top. Count along as they pile up, then make a bunch of your own."
- top-row: "Every grape is a circle. Line up four across the top, each one touching the next."
- second-row: "Tuck three grapes underneath, each one sitting in a gap above."
- last-grapes: "Two more below them, and a single grape at the very bottom. That's ten."
- stalk: "The bunch hangs from a woody stalk. Draw its outline up from the top row to its knobby end."
- branches: "Thin twigs reach down from the stalk to the grapes. Draw them in between."
- leaf: "A grape leaf has three points. Draw one on the left, going up and around all three."
- vein: "One vein runs down the leaf from its middle point."
- tendril: "A tendril is a curly string the vine holds on with. Swing out, loop once, and wiggle off."
- purple: "Color every grape purple."
- shine: "Each grape has a small shiny spot. Color the spots light purple."
- brown: "The stalk is brown."
- green: "Color the leaf green."
- outro: "Ten round grapes and a curly tendril. Would you eat them one at a time, or all at once?"

### pineapple

- intro: "A pineapple wears a spiky crown of leaves, and its skin is covered in diamonds. Body first, then the crown, then the diamonds. Watch it get dressed up, then give yours a crown too."
- body: "The body is a tall oval, like an egg standing up. From the top, curve down the left side."
- body-2: "Then the right side, curving down to meet it at the bottom."
- middle-leaf: "The crown starts with its tallest leaf, standing straight up in the middle."
- inner-leaves: "Add a leaf on each side of it, leaning out a little."
- low-leaves: "Two long leaves curl out low, one to the left and one to the right."
- back-leaves: "Two more leaves poke up from behind. They fill the gaps."
- hatch-one: "Now the diamond pattern. Draw four slanted lines across the body, all leaning the same way."
- hatch-two: "Four more lines lean the other way and cross them. Diamonds everywhere."
- yellow: "Color the body yellow."
- green: "Color most of the leaves green."
- dark-green: "The two leaves beside the middle one are dark green."
- outro: "A spiky crown and diamond skin. Your pineapple looks sweet, and just a little bit prickly."

### fruit-bowl

- intro: "Remember the apple, the pear, the banana and the grapes? They're all back, sharing one big blue bowl. See who sits in front, then fill a bowl of your own."
- rim-pear: "Start with the bowl's front edge, one long curve. The pear sits on it: two sides and a stem."
- apple: "The apple leans against the pear. Round up each side, dip at the top, and add its stem."
- leaf: "A leaf sticks out to the left of the apple's stem. Out and back, then a line through it."
- grapes: "The grapes squeeze in on the right. Draw five round curves, each tucked behind the last."
- grape-stem: "Give the grapes a stem that curls up to the right, and close the little gap."
- banana: "The banana lies along the back. Go up from the leaf, over the top, and cap both ends."
- banana-lines: "Fill in the bits of banana you can see between the fruit, then three long lines along it."
- bowl: "Now finish the bowl: the back rim, the round sides and a little foot. Add a short curve by the grapes."
- blue: "Color the bowl blue, darker on the inside."
- red-green: "Color the apple red and the pear light green."
- yellow-purple: "The banana is yellow and the grapes are purple."
- last-bits: "Stems and banana ends are brown, the leaf green. Add a gray shadow under the bowl."
- outro: "Apple, pear, banana and grapes, all in one bowl you drew. That's the Fruits path done. Anyone hungry?"
