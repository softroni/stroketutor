# In the Air: what Lina says

The words of a path are planned together, so ten lessons in a row do not say the same sentence ten
times, and do not sound like ten different teachers either. This is the plan for the ten `in-the-air`
lessons (Core level), written 2026-09-21 from the pictures the creator kept, before any lesson was
built. Every agent building one of these lessons follows the script below.

The tenth lesson is no longer `busy-sky`. The picture the creator made is a calm sky scene — sun,
cloud, balloon, pine tree, grassy hill — so the lesson is **`balloon-over-the-hill`, "Balloon Over
the Hill"**, objective "Sun, cloud, balloon and pine tree in one scene". Depth by size is gone from
the path; the finale is a scene, as every path's tenth lesson is.

## Rules

1. **US English.** Color, center, gray.
2. **Say what is being drawn, first**, whenever the learner could not guess it. "The canopy is a
   wide dome. From the top, curve down to the left edge." A step that opens with only "Start at the
   top" says where the pen goes and not what is being made. Then, if it helps, where the pen starts
   and one familiar picture (a letter T, a dart, a teardrop).
3. **One or two short sentences, under about twelve words each, 110 characters in all.** Calm, no
   exclamation marks inside steps, no praise, no jargon, no sizes.
4. **Link steps the way a teacher talks:** "Now", "Then", "Carry on". About one step in three, not
   every one.
5. **No sentence is shared word for word with another lesson in the course**, except a plain color
   step. The course already uses these, so they are spent: "Start at the top.", "Go back to the
   top.", "That makes eight.", "Stop before the tip.", "Color the window blue.", "Color the windows
   blue.", "Color the cloud gray.", "Color the leaf green.", "Color the trunk brown.", "Color the
   tires gray, then the hubs cream.", "Draw one line along the middle of the leaf."
   Openers already worn thin across 70 lessons: "From the top,", "From the left", "Begin with the",
   "Start at the", "Go back to", "Draw the same". Use them at most once on this path.
6. **Color steps name the thing and the color**, and vary the frame a little when a lesson has three
   or more: "Color the body cream.", "Both fins are red.", "The windows are gray." Plain is the
   default; never two fancy ones in a row.
7. **Every intro opens differently and ends by handing over in its own words.** Sixty-eight intros
   exist already; none of these ten repeats an opening or a hand-over. "Watch how it comes together,
   then it's your turn." is retired, and so is every "Watch it take shape / See how they join"
   variant listed in `fruits-words.md`.
8. **Outros are one or two short sentences about their drawing**, never about getting it right, and
   never a count that another lesson already ends on.
9. **Titles stay short and plain:** "Draw the body", "Color the wings". Titles may repeat across
   lessons; they are labels, not speech.

## What the pictures gave us

The kept pictures differ from the prompts in a few places, and **the words follow the picture**:

- `hot-air-balloon`: a white skirt hangs between the balloon's mouth and the basket, so there are
  two short skirt lines and no ropes. The balloon has three stripes, red, yellow, red.
- `parachute-crate`: only the top of the canopy is orange; the three panels below the arches are
  white paper, so no color step names them.
- `blimp`: the cabin carries three small gray windows, which the objective does not name. They are
  drawn and colored.
- `airplane`: the nose is on the right, the tail fin at the far left, and the two wings show as one
  below the body and one above it.
- `jet`: the tail ends in a dark gray nozzle with two short lines across it, and one short line
  crosses the nose. Both are drawn.
- `glider`, `drone`, `jet` are seen from straight above; the rest are side or front views.
- `balloon-over-the-hill`: a sun with eight rays, a cloud, the same balloon as lesson 2, a pine tree
  and a grassy hill with four tufts of grass. The hill's line starts and ends at the tree's trunk,
  so the trunk is drawn before the hill.

## The ten scripts

**These are the lines as built** (2026-09-21): where the trace forced a change, the line below is the
one the lesson and the recording actually use. Seven lines differ from the first draft of this plan —
the balloon's basket and the crate (both traced as one closed box), the drone's body (the trace splits
it corner to corner, not down the middle), the jet's engine (the tail's closing line belongs to that
step), and the scene's balloon step and its two last color steps (the pine's top tier is the same
light green as the hill, and coloring the tree before the hill keeps the grass from flashing white).

Step ids and grouping follow the trace; where a trace gives a different grouping, keep the voice of
these lines and adapt. Anything changed is reported back, and all ten are read side by side before
any voice is recorded.

### 1 · paper-airplane

- intro: "Fold a sheet of paper the right way and it flies. This one is three flat triangles meeting at a sharp nose. See how the folds line up, then throw one of your own."
- top-edge: "A paper plane is all straight lines. From the sharp nose, draw one long line down to the left."
- wing-back: "That far corner is the wing tip. Drop a short line down from it."
- fold-top: "Close the top wing with a straight line running back to the nose."
- middle-fold: "The next fold is slimmer. Send a line from the nose to just below the wing tip."
- middle-back: "Join those two lines with a short edge at the back."
- bottom-fold: "The last fold hangs lower. Take its long edge from the nose down to the left."
- bottom-back: "From that low point, come back up to the fold above it."
- color-top: "Color the top wing cream."
- color-middle: "The middle fold is gray."
- color-bottom: "Color the bottom fold blue."
- outro: "Three triangles and one sharp nose. That plane looks ready to sail across a room."

### 2 · hot-air-balloon

- intro: "Balloons fly early, while the air is still. A burner fills the bag and up goes the basket. Watch it take to the sky, then fill one of your own."
- balloon-left: "The balloon is one big teardrop. Set off at the top and curve down its left side."
- balloon-right: "Return to the top and send the same curve down the right."
- mouth: "A straight line across the bottom closes the balloon. That opening is the mouth."
- stripes: "Two long curves split the bag into three stripes, top to bottom."
- skirt: "Under the mouth, two short lines lean in toward the basket."
- basket: "The basket is a little tub. Draw all four sides in one go."
- color-sides: "Color the outer stripes red."
- color-middle: "The middle stripe is yellow."
- color-basket: "Color the basket brown."
- outro: "Your balloon is full and the basket is waiting. Where would you drift off to?"

### 3 · parachute-crate

- intro: "Supplies can be pushed out of a plane and still land gently, once the canopy above them opens. Watch it billow out, then drop a crate of your own."
- dome-left: "The canopy is a wide dome. From its peak, curve down to the left edge."
- dome-right: "Come back to the peak and curve down the right the same way."
- arches: "The bottom of the canopy is three arches, each one curving up."
- cords-outer: "Two long cords hang from the outer tips. Take each one down to a corner of the crate."
- cords-inner: "Two more cords drop from the dips between the arches."
- crate: "The crate is a plain box below. Draw its top line, then its sides and bottom."
- planks: "Two straight lines cross the box like planks."
- color-canopy: "Color the canopy orange."
- color-crate: "Color the crate brown."
- outro: "Canopy open, crate steady. That parcel will land without a bump."

### 4 · blimp

- intro: "A blimp is a balloon with an engine, drifting over stadiums all afternoon. Follow its outline nose to tail, then set yours adrift."
- body-top: "The body is one long oval. Leave the nose and curve over the top to the tail."
- body-bottom: "Now the belly. From the nose again, curve underneath to meet the tail."
- fin-top: "A fin stands near the tail. Draw a slanted four-sided shape leaning backward."
- fin-bottom: "The matching fin hangs below, directly under the first."
- seam: "One long line runs along the middle, clear of both ends."
- cabin: "The cabin hangs under the belly. Draw a small box with rounded bottom corners."
- windows: "Three little windows sit in a row inside it."
- color-body: "Color the body blue."
- color-fins: "Both fins are red."
- color-cabin: "Color the cabin yellow."
- color-windows: "The windows are gray."
- outro: "Long, round and slow. Yours is off over the rooftops now."

### 5 · airplane

- intro: "This is the plane that takes people on vacation, and everything on it hangs off one long tube. Watch the wings go on, then board your own."
- body-top: "The body is a long tube. Run its flat top line from the tail to the nose."
- body-bottom: "Round the nose and bring the belly line back to the tail."
- wing-below: "A wing sweeps back under the middle, hanging below the body."
- wing-above: "The far wing shows above the body, pointing back the same way."
- tail-fin: "The tail fin stands at the very back, leaning toward the tail."
- windows: "Four round windows go in a row along the side."
- color-body: "Color the body cream."
- color-wings: "Color both wings and the tail fin red."
- color-windows: "The windows are blue."
- outro: "Wings on, windows in. Your plane is cleared for the runway."

### 6 · glider

- intro: "A glider carries no engine at all. Long, thin wings hold it up on air that is rising. Look down on it once, then let yours go."
- body-left: "From above, the body is a long teardrop. Draw the nose and its left side."
- body-right: "Back at the nose, take the right side down to the tail."
- wing-left: "A long, thin wing reaches straight out to the left. Both edges stay straight."
- wing-right: "The right wing stretches out just as far."
- tail-wings: "Two short wings sit near the tail, one on each side."
- canopy: "The canopy is a small oval, tucked in behind the nose."
- color-body: "Color the body orange."
- color-wings: "Color all four wings yellow."
- color-canopy: "Color the canopy blue."
- outro: "Two long wings and not a drop of fuel. A glider like that stays up for hours."

### 7 · helicopter

- intro: "A helicopter can stop and hover in one spot, held there by the blade spinning above it. See where that blade sits, then lift off yourself."
- cabin-top: "The cabin is a big egg. Curve from the roof round the nose to the bottom."
- cabin-bottom: "Come back over the roof and round the back to close it."
- boom: "A slim tail boom reaches out behind, from high on the cabin."
- window: "The window is a rounded wedge in the nose. Leave a gap all around."
- mast: "One short upright line stands on the roof."
- blade: "The blade lies across the top of it, like a wide letter T."
- skid: "Below the cabin runs the skid. It curls up at the front like a ski."
- struts: "Two short legs join the belly to the skid."
- color-cabin: "Color the cabin red."
- color-boom: "Color the tail boom orange."
- color-window: "The window is blue."
- outro: "Blade on top, skids underneath. Yours can set down almost anywhere."

### 8 · drone

- intro: "A drone is four rotors and a camera, and it makes most sense seen from straight above. Watch the arms reach out, then send yours up."
- body-left: "The body is a rounded square in the middle. Trace the top and left side first."
- body-right: "Close the square with the right side and the bottom."
- camera: "The camera is one small circle, right at the center."
- arms-top: "Two arms reach out from the top corners, making half a letter X."
- arms-bottom: "Two more go out from the bottom corners."
- rotors-top: "Each arm ends in a rotor. Put a full circle on the two upper ones."
- rotors-bottom: "The lower arms get a circle each as well."
- blades: "One slanted line inside every rotor makes the blade."
- color-body: "Color the body purple."
- color-rotors: "Color the four rotors gray."
- color-camera: "The camera is blue."
- outro: "Four rotors and one small camera. Straight up, and over the trees."

### 9 · jet

- intro: "Fast jets are built like darts: a pointed nose and wings swept right back. See what this one leaves behind, then let yours streak past."
- body-top: "From above, the jet is a dart. Draw the pointed nose and the top edge."
- body-bottom: "Set out from the nose again and run the bottom edge back."
- wings: "Both wings sweep back from the middle, one up, one down."
- tail-wings: "Two smaller wings sweep back at the tail, above and below."
- canopy: "The canopy is an oval behind the nose. One short line crosses the tip."
- nozzle: "One line closes the tail. Two more cross the engine, where it blows."
- trail: "Two straight lines float behind it: the trail it leaves in the sky."
- color-body: "Color the body gray."
- color-wings: "Color all four wings red."
- color-canopy: "Color the canopy blue."
- color-nozzle: "The engine end is dark gray."
- outro: "Wings swept back, trail stretching out. Nothing on this path flies faster."

### 10 · balloon-over-the-hill

- intro: "Everything is calm up here. A sun, a cloud, a balloon and one pine tree on a green hill. Watch the sky fill up piece by piece, then make it yours."
- sun: "The sun is a circle high on the left. Four rays point up, down and out to the sides."
- rays: "Four more rays fill the gaps, so the sun points all around."
- cloud: "A cloud floats along beside it: three soft bumps on top, flat underneath."
- balloon-sides: "The balloon hangs at the right. Curve down one side, then the other, then the basket."
- balloon-lines: "Two curves stripe the bag. Two straight lines close the mouth and basket."
- tree: "A pine tree stands on the left: three triangles and a short trunk."
- hill: "The hill is one long curve. It runs out from the trunk to both edges."
- grass-left: "Tufts of grass poke up out of the hill. Draw two of them on the left."
- grass-right: "Two more tufts sprout over on the right."
- color-yellow: "Color the sun yellow, and the balloon's middle stripe as well."
- color-red: "The two side stripes are red."
- color-tree: "The pine's lower branches and the grass are dark green. Trunk and basket are brown."
- color-cloud-hill: "Last, color the cloud gray. The hill and the tree's top are light green."
- outro: "A whole afternoon on one page: sun, cloud, hill and a balloon going somewhere. That is the In the Air path finished."
