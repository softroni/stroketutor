# Wheels: what Lina says

The words of a path are planned together, so ten lessons in a row do not say the same sentence ten
times, and do not sound like ten different teachers either. This is the plan for the `wheels` lessons
(Core level, 2026-09-21), written from the pictures as they came back, not from the prompts. The models
to follow are the creator's own Strawberry and the scripts in [fruits-words.md](fruits-words.md),
[food-treats-words.md](food-treats-words.md), [forms-words.md](forms-words.md),
[plants-words.md](plants-words.md) and [sky-weather-words.md](sky-weather-words.md).

The creator replaced the planned last lesson, `car-three-quarter-view`, with an `ambulance` (2026-09-21):
its picture arrived with the other nine, without a prompt of ours. It closes the path.

Wheels is the path where things are built, so Lina talks like someone putting a machine together:
the big body first, then what is fixed to it, the wheels tucked under their arches last. The three
machines that are mostly wheel (monster truck, bicycle, motorcycle) start with the wheels instead.

## Rules

1. **US English.** Color, center, gray, tire.
2. **Say what is being drawn, first.** "The bus is a long box." "The arm has an elbow." A step never
   opens with only "Start at the top". Then, if it helps, where the pen starts and one familiar
   picture (a table, a hill, a rainbow, a pill, a plus sign, a letter T, a walking cane).
3. **One or two short sentences, under about twelve words each, 110 characters in all.** Calm, no
   exclamation marks inside steps, no praise, no jargon, no sizes. Say "arch" for a wheel arch, "tire"
   for the outer circle, "hub" for the inner one, "frame" for the bar under a truck or train, "bed" for
   the dump truck's box, "tracks" for the excavator's crawler, "treads" for the monster truck's tire marks.
4. **Link steps the way a teacher talks:** "Now", "Next", "Then", "One more". About one step in three.
5. **No sentence is shared word for word with another lesson of any path, except a plain color
   step.** Wheels, hubs, arches and windows come back in almost every lesson here, so each script
   words them differently; keep those differences.
6. **Color steps name the thing and the color.** Plain is the default; at most one per lesson is
   framed differently. Two small areas may share a color step, named in the order to color them. Lina
   says "black" for the charcoal tires, "green" for leaf green, "dark green", "cream".
7. **Intros each open differently and end by handing over, in different words.** Outros are one or
   two short sentences about their drawing, never about getting it right.
8. **Titles stay short and plain:** "Draw the roof", "Draw the tires", "Color the cab". Titles may
   repeat across lessons; they are labels, not speech.
9. **Drawing rules still hold** (see the author-lesson skill): straight edges stay whole, but a long
   body outline is cut at its corners into a few comfortable lines (roof and ends, then the floor with
   its arches); a big closed curve is split where a pen would lift (a corner, a dip; on a plain big
   circle, top and bottom); small closed shapes (a window, a hub, a tire under 850 units around) stay
   whole; one thing is finished before the next; at most six lines in a step; a line that is fixed to
   something starts where it joins it; long level lines run left to right.

## Phrases already used by published lessons (do not reuse)

Everything listed under the same heading in [plants-words.md](plants-words.md) and
[forms-words.md](forms-words.md), and every script in the five words documents. In particular:

- Openers: "Today we're drawing …", "In this lesson we're drawing …", "Hi, Lina here.", "Think of a
  beach.", "Think of a party hat.", "Flash.", "Surf's up.", "Rumble, rumble.", "Psst.", "Lunch is
  served.", "Pizza night.", "Two scoops, please.", "Who wants a donut?", "Roll the dice."
- Hand-overs: "Watch how it comes together, then it's your turn.", "… then grab your pen.", "… then pick
  up your pen.", "… then it's over to you.", "… then you try.", "… then it's your go.", "… then build
  yours.", "… then have a go.", "… then try it.", "… then it's your pen's turn.", "… then roll your
  own.", "… then wrap yours.", "… then stack your own.", "… then set up your own.", "… then plant one on
  your page.", "… then grow your own.", "… then raise your own.", "… then start your garden.", "… then
  strike.", "… then make it rain.", "… then let it snow.", "… then spin your own.", "… then ride your
  own.", "… then climb your own.", "… then bring on the storm."
- Step sentences: "Draw one line along the middle of the leaf. Stop before the tip.", "Start at the
  top. Draw a big letter C down to the bottom.", "Go back to the top. Draw the same curve the other
  way, like a mirror.", "Draw a small circle right in the middle." (orange-half).
- Outros: "It looks like you could pick it up.", "What flavor is inside?", "You can almost hear the
  waves.", "Can you hear it crash?", "Did you hear the thunder?", "Hold on to your hat.", every "That's
  the … path, finished/done."

## The scripts

Step ids and grouping follow the trace; where a lesson's trace gives a different grouping, keep the
voice of these lines and adapt the count. Anything changed is reported back, and all scripts are read
side by side before the creator reviews them.

### school-bus

(Picture: yellow box with two arches, four blue windows, one floating stripe, solid black tires with
gray hubs. Black tires have no outline of their own, so the tire and hub circles come from the
color-edge trace, merged into `school-bus.ready.json`. Cut the body at its four corners: back end,
roof and front end are one line like a table; the floor with its two arches is the other, left to right.)

- intro: "Beep beep, here comes the school bus. It's one long box with four windows and two wheels. Watch it pull up, then hop on with your pen."
- box: "The bus is a long box. From the bottom left, go up, along the roof and down, like a wide table."
- floor: "Now the floor. Run along the bottom and hop over two round arches, one near each end."
- windows-left: "Windows are squares with soft corners. Draw two of them, side by side."
- windows-right: "Two more to the right, and the row of four is full."
- stripe: "A long stripe runs under the windows. Stop a little before each end."
- tires: "Tuck a tire under each arch. It's a circle that leaves a thin gap all around."
- hubs: "Give each tire a hub, a small circle in its center."
- colors: "Color the bus yellow." / "Color the windows blue." / "The tires are black." / "Color the hubs gray."
- outro: "Four windows, two wheels and a stripe down the side. Next stop, your school."

### car

(Picture: one smooth orange body with two dips in its top line, two blue quarter-round windows, gray
tires and cream hubs in arches. Traces clean. The body is 2,400 units around: cut it at the back
bottom corner, the two dips and the front bottom corner, giving trunk, roof, hood and floor.)

- intro: "This little car is all curves: a bump for the trunk, a dome for the roof, a bump for the hood. See the three bumps join up, then take yours for a spin."
- trunk: "The back of the car is a low bump. From the bottom left, curve up and over to the dip."
- roof: "Next, the roof. It's a big dome from this dip to the next one, like a hill."
- hood: "One more bump makes the hood. Curve over and down to the bottom right."
- floor: "Close the car underneath. Go straight along, with an arch over each wheel."
- window-back: "The back window fits under the dome. It's straight below and beside, curved on top."
- window-front: "The front window is its mirror, across a strip of orange."
- tires: "A tire sits inside each arch without touching it. Draw both circles."
- hubs: "Each tire needs a small circle inside, the hub."
- colors: "Color the car orange." / "Color the windows blue." / "Color the tires gray." / "Color the hubs cream."
- outro: "Round roof, round wheels, and not one sharp corner. Where will you drive it first?"

### dump-truck

with a blue window standing on the frame's right end; a long green bed tilted up toward the cab, its
low corner resting near the frame's back end, with four rib lines from edge to edge; one floating
lift-arm line; gray tires, cream hubs. Differs from the prompt in colors and ribs; the words follow
the picture. Traces clean. The frame is 2,100 around: cut it into a top line and a bottom line. The
bed is straight-sided: cut it at two opposite corners into two L-shaped lines. Ribs run from the
bed's top edge down to its bottom edge.)

- intro: "A dump truck has one great trick: its bed tips up and the load slides out the back. Ours is caught mid-tip. See what holds the bed up, then tip your own."
- frame-top: "Everything rides on the frame. Draw its top, a long line with a low bump over each wheel."
- frame-bottom: "Now the underside. Close the frame, arching over both wheels."
- cab: "The cab stands on the front of the frame. Go up, across the roof and down."
- window: "The driver needs a window. Draw a square with soft corners, high in the cab."
- bed-top: "The bed is a long box, tipped up. From its low corner, draw the short end and the long top."
- bed-bottom: "Then the high end and the long bottom, back to where you began."
- ribs: "Four ribs make the bed strong. Draw each one straight across, top edge to bottom edge."
- arm: "One short leaning line holds the bed up. Start on the frame and stop below the bed." (changed in the build: the traced arm stands on the frame)
- wheels: "Each wheel is two circles, a tire in its arch and a hub inside that."
- colors: "Color the bed green." / "Color the cab red." / "Color the frame brown." / "Color the window blue." / "Color the tires gray, then the hubs cream."

### excavator

at the two ends. The trace runs the stick's inner side on into the bucket: cut it where the bucket

- intro: "An excavator is a digging machine with one long arm that bends at an elbow, like yours. Tracks first, then the cab, then the arm. Watch it reach out, then dig in."

- tracks-bottom: "Now the bottom half, back along the ground."
- track-wheels: "Three wheels turn inside the tracks. Draw three circles in a row."
- body: "The body is a low box sitting on the tracks. Its top dips where the arm will stand."
- cab: "The cab stands on the back of the body. Up, over with soft corners, and down."

### monster-truck

(Picture: two giant gray tires with cream hubs and eight tread lines each, a small purple pickup body
riding above them on two wide arches, one blue window. In the picture the treads grow inward from the
tire's circle, so the ink trace broke both circles into pieces. `monster-truck.ready.json` has each
tire circle from the color-edge trace, moved onto the middle of its ink, and the sixteen treads
measured from the picture's ink, each running from the circle inward. Each tire is 1,130 around: cut
top and bottom. Treads go on like a clock: up, down, left, right, then the four in between.)

- intro: "Monster trucks are nearly all wheel. We start with two giant tires, give them chunky treads, and set a small truck on top. Watch the wheels go on first, then go big."
- tire-left: "Start with a giant tire. Draw the left half of the circle, then the right half."
- tire-right: "The second tire is the same size. Leave a gap between the two."
- hubs: "Each tire has a big round hub. Draw a circle in the middle of both."
- treads-left-plus: "Treads are short lines that point at the hub. On the left tire, draw four like a plus sign."
- treads-left-x: "Then four more in between, like a letter X. That makes eight."
- treads-right-plus: "Same again on the right tire. Top, bottom, left and right."
- treads-right-x: "Fill in its four slanted treads."
- body-top: "Now the truck. Draw a low bed, step up over the cab, and slope down the hood."
- body-bottom: "Underneath, swoop over each tire in a wide arch. Keep a gap above the rubber."
- window: "The cab gets one square window."
- colors: "Color the truck purple." / "Color both tires gray." / "Color the hubs cream." / "Color the window blue."
- outro: "Giant tires, tiny truck. Nothing is going to get in its way."

### bicycle

(Picture: two plain circles, two small gray hubs, a brown saddle on a seat post, a level handlebar on
a stem; frame lines from the back hub forward to the pedal point and up to the seat corner, a seat
tube, a level top tube, a slanted down tube, and a slightly curved fork down to the front hub. The
trace runs several tubes together as one line (back hub, pedal point, steering corner, front hub):
cut them at every corner, so each tube is its own straight line. (After the build the five tubes and the stem were refitted as straight lines from the trace, and both hubs replaced by clean circles: the tracer had kinked the steering corner.) The back hub's circle was broken by
the tubes that meet it; `bicycle.ready.json` has it from the color-edge trace. Each wheel is 1,010
around: cut top and bottom. Only two colors. Zoom into every corner for tails.)

- intro: "A bicycle is two circles and a handful of straight lines. There's hardly any coloring, so every line counts. Watch how the lines meet at the corners, then ride along."
- back-wheel: "The back wheel is a big circle. Draw one half, then the other."
- front-wheel: "The front wheel matches it. Leave room between them for the frame."
- hubs: "Mark the center of each wheel with a tiny circle."
- seat-tube: "The frame begins with one slanted line, from under the seat down to where the pedals go."
- back-triangle: "From the back hub, draw one line to the top of that tube and one to the bottom."
- main-triangle: "Next, a level bar forward from the seat, and a slanted bar up from the pedals to meet it."
- fork: "The fork holds the front wheel. From above the bars, come down through the corner to the front hub."
- handlebars: "Lay the handlebars flat across the top, like the top of a T."
- saddle: "The seat is a small wedge, round at the back and pointed at the front."
- colors: "Color the seat brown." / "Color the hubs gray."
- outro: "Two circles and nine lines, and it really is a bicycle. Lean it somewhere safe."

### motorcycle

- exhaust: "The exhaust is a long pipe from behind the seat, out over the back tire. Round off its end."
- fork: "One strong line is the fork. From the front hub, go up past the tank, then bend back for the handlebar."
- colors: "Color the engine purple." / "Color the tank red." / "Color the seat brown." / "Color the exhaust yellow." / "Color the tires gray, then the hubs cream."
