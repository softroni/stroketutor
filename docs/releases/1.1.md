## What's in it

Already on `main` since 1.0 (2):

- **Urban**: an eleventh path (shop fronts, balconies, a canal bridge, a café…).
- **Photos in dim light**: Original, Bright or Scan for a photographed page (`a7afe0b`).
- **Sketchbook**: a kept page's light and corners can be changed later (`787e012`).
- **Completion**: the time the drawing really took, and the screen stays awake (`65b120a`).
- **Path colors**: every path names its color (`29372d8`).
- **Premium**: a tap on a crowned lesson opens the paywall, every time; a child gets the grown-up check first
  (`d42308c`). The drawer and the child's wish-list button are gone.
- **iPad**: the teacher beside the paper, and paths that keep one column until the nodes have room.
- **Premium**: any product in its subscription group unlocks it, and "Your free week has started" names the price
  of the product the week began on (`508ec63`, docs/next-builds.md item 1).
- **Crash and hang reports** from MetricKit to PostHog (`a7e1d28`). App Privacy has had Crash Data and Performance
  Data (not linked, App Functionality) since 2026-09-30.
- **Rating prompt**: learners 13 and over are asked for an App Store rating after a finished drawing, from
  their third on, never in their first session, at most once per version (`RatingPromptPolicy`,
  docs/next-builds.md item 4); PostHog gets `rating_prompt_requested`. Never for a child.
- Plus item 5 of docs/next-builds.md, and the Café, once merged.

## The release checklist (Claude)

1. `git worktree add ../stroketutor-release-1.1 -b release/1.1 <the commit on main>`; `MARKETING_VERSION` 1.1,
   `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` 4 (1 and 2 are used; 3 was never uploaded).
2. Tests: the iOS suite and `web/` (`npx vitest run`), all green.
3. On the iPhone and iPad simulators: the first run, a lesson, the three photo modes, a sketchbook edit, both
   paywalls (18+ through Superwall, a child through the grown-up check), restore, and the rating prompt's rules.
   Events reach PostHog (`build: debug`).
4. Screenshots as above.
5. Archive into `~/Library/Developer/Xcode/Archives` (kept: its dSYM symbolicates crash reports), export with the
   profile **Paper Coach App Store** (installed from App Store Connect 2026-09-30), upload, wait for VALID.
6. App Store Connect: version 1.1, the build, what's new, description, promotional text, keywords, review notes,
   **phased release on**, submit. Screenshots with `upload.py --apply`.
7. When Apple approves: tag `1.1(4)` and push it, merge `release/1.1` into `main`, log it, and tick
   docs/next-builds.md items 1 and 4 with the version. Then Claude proposes the price test's products to Kevin
   (item 2).
